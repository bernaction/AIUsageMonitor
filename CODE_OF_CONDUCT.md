# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AI Usage Gadget a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and help them contribute successfully.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, trolling, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, reject, or remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and whenever someone is publicly representing the project.
